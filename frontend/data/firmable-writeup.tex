\section{Discovery and triage}
Ranked 50 datasets from 1,509 deduplicated CKAN records. Outputs retain IDs, titles, publishers, formats, scores and reasons. The 100-point rubric uses business evidence (45), valid identifiers (20), corporate-name patterns (10), business names (10), identifier--name agreement (10) and catalogue category (5); it is not a probability.\par
Agentic triage assesses actual file previews instead of relying on potentially ambiguous descriptions. Human review confirmed all 20 sampled resources: 0/20 false positives (0\% in this sample). The demo shows up to 50 rows per audited resource.\par
\section{Schema inference and mapping}
Six sources provide complementary identity, purpose, financial, service and regulatory data across ZIP/TSV, XLSX, CSV and JSON. ASIC Credit and another ATO year extend coverage to eight extracts/seven families:\par
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{ASIC Companies.} Identifiers, current/former names, registration, legal form and status.
\item \textbf{ACNC.} Charitable purpose, beneficiaries, size bands, websites, contact addresses and operating regions.
\item \textbf{ATO, two years.} Total income, taxable income and tax payable, with reporting periods.
\item \textbf{DEWR.} Employment-service programmes, provider sites, addresses and contacts.
\item \textbf{QBCC.} Contractor identity, licence categories, grades and addresses.
\item \textbf{Victorian Liquor.} Licensees, permits, licensed premises and capacity.
\item \textbf{ASIC Credit.} Credit-licence status, dates, permissions and conditions.
\end{itemize}
One extractor read 116/168 columns: 85,446 observations from 7,744 saved rows. Roles separate company facts, contact addresses, sites and licences; unsupported fields remain unmapped. Eight mappings are human-verified, including seven maintainer-corrected configurations: an exception to fully agent-authored mapping.\par
\section{Entity identification}
Threshold 0.85 yields 1,455 links across 793 multi-source entities (736 links within the original six sources). Each link retains both source-record IDs, canonical key, confidence and evidence. The 1,245-record unlinked queue explains refusals: weak evidence, ambiguity or conflicting identifiers.\par
Constrained clusters use ABN, then ACN, then source-record identity as their key anchor; conflicting valid identifiers block merges. Separate licence, site and programme edges preserve one-to-many relationships. Parent/subsidiary ownership is withheld without direct evidence.\par
Human-confirmed review found 46/50 links correct (92\%). This rule-stratified, overlap-enriched sample is not a population estimate. Four failures remain flagged in the proposed output; confidence scores are policy scores, not calibrated probabilities.\par
\section{Company profiles}
Fifty profiles combine identity, activities, locations, licences and period-specific finances. Linked-source examples:\par
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{The Personnel Group Ltd.} ACNC size and operating regions plus 11 DEWR establishment records reveal its documented service footprint.
\item \textbf{WGA Group Holdings Pty Ltd.} ASIC identity plus two ATO years show total income rising from AUD 138.65m to 145.31m; these are tax measures, not profit.
\item \textbf{Chinese Association of Victoria Inc.} Charitable purpose combines with a licensed-premises record and BYO permit.
\end{itemize}
Core/financial claims retain confidence, provenance, alternatives and selection reasons. Reliability-weighted evidence, bounded corroboration and recency determine winners; unsupported facts are absent. Nested site/licence fields retain observation references but still lack complete merged-confidence objects.\par
Removing ASIC changes 26/50 profiles: 210 field entries disappear, 10 values change and 20 profiles lose multi-source support. Confidence-only changes are excluded. All 25 financially populated profiles depend on ATO; uncorroborated source errors can survive.\par

\vspace{4pt}
\noindent\textbf{2. Agent design.}\par
Inspect files, test readers, profile samples, propose mappings, validate held-out rows, critique semantics, revise, then seek human approval. This separates format, execution and interpretation errors. Provider errors retry; exhausted runs checkpoint. Humans see rules, samples, confidence, unmapped fields and validation/critic findings. Semantics is weakest: seven executable mappings needed corrections, including contact addresses treated as sites.\par
\vspace{4pt}
\noindent\textbf{3. The hard one.}\par
ASIC supplied a ZIP with tab-separated .csv data and historical-name rows. Reader settings and conditional mappings handle both. Use a custom reader only when the shared configuration cannot represent a format reliably; preserve the canonical output contract.\par
\vspace{4pt}
\noindent\textbf{4. Scale.}\par
Acquisition fails first: three of nine initial attempts hit HTTP errors or bot challenges; QBCC's fallback was a biased 32k-row prefix. At 500 sources, add publisher queues, pagination, caching, retry limits and schema/freshness checks. Review needs prioritisation; matching needs persistent candidate indexes and incremental processing.\par
\vspace{4pt}
\noindent\textbf{5. Change.}\par
Preserve immutable snapshots/observations, mapping/model/policy versions, candidate evidence, scores, refusals, memberships and human decisions. Run both matchers on identical evidence; compare changed links and cluster splits/merges. Evaluate frozen human labels, review high-impact differences and promote gradually. Rebuild affected profiles, retain old memberships for rollback and publish key aliases/tombstones. Reuse human decisions only while evidence remains applicable.\par
The implemented comparison retains 1,205 links, adds 250 and removes 363. Evidence-bound review journals exist; automatic corrections, persistent membership history and production rollback remain future work.\par
\vspace{4pt}
\noindent\textbf{6. Next.}\par
Week 1: regressions for four failed links and reviewed cluster corrections. Week 2: reliable fetching, schema-drift detection and review/resume controls. Week 3: nested-field confidence, financial-corruption testing and matcher migration with rollback. Correct known errors before scaling.\par
